Pixel Beans (豆豆开炮) for iPhone

Tap the beans at the bottom: each one rides the belt around the picture and shoots the outermost blocks of its own color. Clear every block to finish the picture. New rules arrive one at a time, each with a card that explains it; you can reread them any time under Settings → How It Works.

Questions and bug reports

Please open an issue on GitHub with your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number. We read every report.

Your progress

Progress is saved only on your device. It is not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

Privacy

The game is fully offline and collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.

支持

豆豆开炮（Pixel Beans）iPhone 版

点下面的豆豆，它会坐上传送带绕画一圈，打掉最外层、和自己同色的方块；把方块全部打掉，画就拼完了。新玩法会一个一个登场，每个都有说明卡片，之后也能在「设置 → 玩法说明」里随时再看。

问题和反馈

请在 GitHub 上提交 Issue，写上手机型号、iOS 版本和关卡号，我们都会看。

游戏进度

进度只保存在你的设备上，不会在设备之间同步；删除 App 会一并删除。

隐私

游戏完全离线，不收集任何个人数据，详见隐私政策。
