Privacy Policy

Pixel Beans (豆豆开炮) · Last updated: September 27, 2026

Pixel Beans is an offline puzzle game. It needs no account and does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps your level progress, stars, coins, boosters, daily-challenge streak and settings (sound, music, speed, color assist) on your device. None of it is sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Photos

If you choose to turn a photo into a level, you pick it with the system photo picker, which gives the game only the photo you select; the game does not ask for access to your photo library. The photo is converted into pixel art on your device, is not saved by the game and never leaves your device.

Network access

The game does not use the network. Levels, daily challenges and all features work fully offline.

Other permissions

The game does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, health data or files.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds any processing of personal data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please see the support page.

隐私政策

豆豆开炮（Pixel Beans）· 最后更新：2026 年 9 月 27 日

豆豆开炮是一款离线解谜游戏，不需要注册账号，不收集、不传输、不出售、不分享任何个人数据；游戏里没有广告、没有统计分析，也没有任何第三方 SDK。

保存在你设备上的数据

游戏在你的设备上保存关卡进度、星星、金币、道具、每日挑战连续天数和设置（音效、音乐、速度、色盲辅助）。这些数据不会发给开发者或任何人；删除 App 就会一并删除。

照片

如果你想把照片做成关卡，需要通过系统照片选择器挑选，游戏只能拿到你选的那一张，不会申请访问你的相册。照片在你的设备上转换成像素画，游戏不会保存它，它也不会离开你的设备。

网络

游戏不使用网络，关卡、每日挑战和所有功能都能完全离线使用。

其他权限

游戏不访问通讯录、位置、相机、麦克风、健康数据或文件。

儿童

游戏不收集任何个人数据，因此也不会收集儿童的数据。

变更

如果以后的版本需要处理个人数据，会在该版本发布之前更新本政策。

联系

对本政策有疑问，请看支持页。
